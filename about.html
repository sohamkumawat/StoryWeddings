At Storyteller Weddings, we see weddings not merely as sequences of rituals, but as living tapestries of human connection, heritage, and genuine love.

Where It Began

Rooted in Pune, Documenting Across India

Storyteller Weddings was founded on a singular passion: turning fleeting, unrepeatable wedding celebrations into cinematic heirlooms. Based in the cultural hub of Pune, Maharashtra, our lenses have traversed royal forts in Rajasthan, serene coastal horizons in Goa, and grand urban ballrooms in Mumbai.

We reject rigid, awkward posing. Instead, our team blends quietly into your wedding celebration — capturing the proud tear in a father’s eye, the shared secret smiles during the pheras, and the untamed dance floor energy.

Our Creative Vision

The Fine-Art Editorial Approach

Every couple possesses a unique rhythm. Whether it is a vibrant Maharashtrian ceremony adorned with nauvari elegance and Peshwai charm, or a contemporary sunset vows exchange, we bring an editorial perspective to your celebrations.

Each photograph is meticulously color-graded with rich, natural skin tones and deep cinematic contrast, ensuring that your photo book feels contemporary today and heirloom-worthy fifty years from now.
